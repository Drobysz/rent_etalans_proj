# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Rental agency operators who manage paid add-on services for short-term housing bookings. They use the admin panel during routine back-office work: reviewing orders, checking payment details, and preparing service catalog changes.

## Product Purpose

The product sells additional services for short-term rental guests and gives staff a focused admin surface for services and orders. Success means staff can scan current data quickly, open payment details without losing context, and prepare future API integration cleanly.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, practical. The interface should feel like a dependable internal tool, closer to Linear, Raycast, and GitHub than a marketing dashboard.

## Anti-references

No hero sections, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, oversized radius, ornamental badges, floating shells, dramatic shadows, generic SaaS copy, or transform-heavy hover effects.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize scanning over decoration.
- Keep navigation predictable and compact.
- Make payment details complete but contained.
- Separate data access, layout, and presentation.
- Use restrained motion only to clarify state changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast for body text and controls, keyboard-operable modal controls, Escape and backdrop dismissal for dialogs, visible focus states, and reduced-motion handling for UI transitions.
